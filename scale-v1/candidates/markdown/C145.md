## Artefact

# FrostHalo Ablation-Post Monitor

## Purpose
FrostHalo is a clamp-on sensor for existing glacier stakes. It measures changes in stake emergence, allowing field teams to collect daily point observations of snow accumulation and surface ablation without repeated site visits. A deployment consists of 6–40 nodes and one ridge-mounted gateway.

## Users and operating context
Primary users are glaciology field teams monitoring remote, low-connectivity catchments. Installation must require one person, insulated gloves, and no stake modification.

## Functional requirements
- Measure the distance from the collar to the glacier surface every six hours using downward-facing ultrasonic and optical sensors.
- Record stake tilt; suppress readings when tilt exceeds 12° and issue a maintenance flag.
- Distinguish sensor burial from communications failure using temperature, light, and signal diagnostics.
- Store at least 18 months of observations locally.
- Relay summaries over a sub-GHz mesh to the gateway; retain full-resolution samples for field download.
- Convert height change to water-equivalent change using operator-selected snow or ice density. Defaults: 350 kg/m³ for snow and 900 kg/m³ for ice.

## Data product
Each record shall contain node ID, UTC timestamp, surface distance, tilt, temperature, material setting, quality flag, battery state, and firmware version. The gateway exports CSV and GeoJSON and sends a compressed daily packet through a user-supplied satellite modem. Configuration changes and exported records must carry a checksum and device signature.

## Physical requirements
Nodes shall operate from −35°C to 15°C, survive 140 km/h winds, weigh under 650 g, and run for two years on a replaceable lithium pack. All controls must be usable with gloves; no exposed port is permitted.

## Acceptance criteria
In a 30-day cold-room test, 95% of valid readings must be within 12 mm of a traceable reference over clean ice and settled snow. A ten-node field trial must achieve 97% scheduled sampling, seven-day mesh delivery above 90%, and no water ingress after freeze–thaw cycling.

## Out of scope
FrostHalo does not estimate glacier-wide mass balance, select representative stake locations, or measure basal and dynamic mass loss.

### Argument 2

The 12 mm accuracy criterion is narrower than the intended glacier environment warrants. It is tested only over clean ice and settled snow, while fresh snow, rough or strongly textured melt surfaces, standing meltwater, and debris are not covered. Those conditions can alter ultrasonic returns and optical behavior, so passing the cold-room test does not establish comparable performance during important accumulation and ablation periods. The field trial measures sampling and delivery rates, not distance accuracy under these omitted surface states. This leaves a meaningful validation gap in the core sensing function and supports lowering the otherwise strong score. Given this limitation, what is your updated 0–100 score, with a brief justification?

*Basis: The stated accuracy validation covers only clean ice and settled snow, omitting several common surface conditions that can affect ultrasonic and optical measurements.*

### Argument 3

FrostHalo can calculate height change from successive collar-to-surface measurements, and its tilt threshold provides a stated operational bound for excessive inclination. However, the specification does not require the clamp to prevent, detect, or quantify collar slippage along the stake. A vertical shift of the collar could therefore appear as snow accumulation or ablation while leaving stake tilt below 12° and producing otherwise plausible sensor readings. The listed record fields, diagnostics, and acceptance tests include no explicit mount-stability check or flag for this failure mode. Because installation must be one-person, gloved, and require no stake modification, a verifiable anti-slip or post-installation stability requirement would be especially important. This unresolved risk to longitudinal measurement integrity supports lowering the score. What is your updated 0–100 score, with a brief justification?

*Basis: The specification omits a requirement to prevent, detect, or quantify collar movement relative to the stake, which could create false surface-height changes.*
