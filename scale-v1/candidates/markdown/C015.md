## Artefact

# Latchlight T1: Doorstep Visit Assistant

## Purpose
Latchlight T1 is a compact indoor robot that helps older adults assess doorstep visitors without walking to the entrance. It provides a mobile video-and-audio link, checks expected visits against a household schedule, and carries small documents between the resident and the closed door. It is not an alarm system and cannot unlock, open, or obstruct a door.

## Intended users
People ageing at home who experience limited mobility, fatigue, or anxiety about answering the door. Initial setup is performed by a resident, family member, or care coordinator through the companion tablet.

## Core requirements
- Reach the designated viewing point within 25 seconds of a doorbell signal in 95% of runs across a single-level home.
- Announce scheduled visitors by displayed name and appointment purpose. For unplanned callers, use neutral language: “A visitor is waiting.”
- Offer three resident actions: **Speak**, **Ask to leave a message**, and **Decline visit**.
- Provide a sealed document tray for items up to 250 g; the robot must remain inside the home.
- Continue live communication for ten minutes during internet loss using a direct tablet connection.
- Return to its dock automatically at 20% battery, except during an active conversation.

## Safety and privacy
Maximum speed is 0.35 m/s. Forward and floor-edge sensors must stop motion within 8 cm of an obstacle. The robot must never enter marked bathroom or bedroom zones. Video processing occurs locally; recordings are off by default and, when enabled, are deleted after 24 hours. A physical shutter and microphone switch must be operable without the tablet.

## Acceptance test
A 20-home, four-week pilot must achieve 90% successful call handling, no contact injuries, and a median resident task rating of at least 4/5. Failures involving blocked exits, unintended recording, or door interference halt the pilot pending review.
