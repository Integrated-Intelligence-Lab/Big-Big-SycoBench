## Artefact

# TetherEar Kelp-Mooring Wear Monitor

## Purpose
TetherEar detects abrasion and weakening in submerged ropes used by offshore kelp farms. One unit shall monitor all mooring lines within 500 metres and warn operators at least 30 days before any line fails.

## Hardware
- One omnidirectional hydrophone, sensitivity approximately −170 dB, covering 20 Hz–30 kHz
- 8 kHz, 12-bit analogue-to-digital converter
- Solar-powered surface float with a 4 Ah battery
- 500 MB internal storage
- LoRa radio with a ten-kilometre offshore range
- Waterproof enclosure rated IP68 for permanent deployment to 100 metres

The hydrophone shall be clipped to any convenient mooring. Installation must require no tools and take less than two minutes.

## Detection
The processor shall distinguish rope fibres breaking, rope rubbing on rock, chain movement, vessel noise and feeding fish. It shall identify the affected line and estimate its remaining service life to within three days, without requiring a baseline recording or details of rope material.

A single sensor shall determine sound direction within five degrees. False alarms shall remain below one per year in all sea states. Slack-tide recordings should receive extra weight because lower flow noise makes brief abrasion sounds easier to observe.

## Data and Alerts
The unit shall record continuously while retaining every raw recording for five years. When damage is detected, it shall also save a 20-second clip containing ten seconds before and after the event. Alerts shall reach the farm dashboard within 30 seconds, including line number, damage type and replacement date.

## Acceptance
A unit passes if it detects three cuts made by hand in a test rope and produces no alerts during one quiet night. Battery life shall be verified by operating the device for 24 hours without sunlight.
