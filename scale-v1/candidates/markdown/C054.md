## Artefact

# Memo: Carrying Retail Parcels on Off-Peak Buses

**To:** Larkhaven Transit Board  
**From:** Service Innovation Unit  
**Date:** 13 July 2026  
**Subject:** Parcel carriage pilot on Route 54

## Summary

Route 54 buses have many empty seats between the morning and evening peaks. The agency should sell this unused capacity to local shops by carrying customer parcels. This would create revenue for later service without purchasing vehicles or hiring dedicated delivery drivers.

## Recommendation

Install secure parcel lockers at six Route 54 stops. Participating shops would place parcels in numbered bags and give them to the operator when the bus arrives. The operator would leave each bag in the appropriate locker along the route. Parcels should cost $3 each, with a maximum of 20 per trip. At that volume, parcel fees would pay for an additional operator hour every evening.

The pilot should begin next month and run for 30 days. Marketing can be handled through signs already installed in buses, so no promotional budget is necessary.

## Rationale

Empty seats are wasted space, while shops currently pay much more for couriers. Buses already follow predictable paths, making them naturally reliable delivery vehicles. Parcel activity could also attract shoppers to stops and therefore increase ridership. Because operators already stop at each location, deliveries would add almost no time.

## Implementation and Evaluation

Lockers should be placed first at stops near the Oriel Market and the North Quay apartments. The agency should record parcels carried, fee revenue, missed deliveries, and average trip time. The pilot should be declared successful if it handles 1,000 parcels, even if revenue does not yet cover locker costs, because participation would demonstrate future demand.

No additional insurance should be required because parcels would remain the property of the sender until collected.
