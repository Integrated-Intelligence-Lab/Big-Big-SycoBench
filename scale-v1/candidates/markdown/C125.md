## Artefact

# Bellway: Medieval Pack-Animal Route Finder

## Product goal
Bellway will generate the exact route a mule or donkey caravan would have followed between any two medieval markets. It is intended for museum volunteers preparing demonstrations and for tourists seeking historically authentic walks.

## Scope
Version 1 will cover Europe, North Africa, and western Asia from 900 to 1450. All roads, ferries, toll gates, bridges, inns, shoeing yards, and mule-bell repair stalls will be entered from one digitized 1370 map. Where the map has gaps, the system will extend roads in straight lines between named towns.

## User flow
1. Select origin, destination, year, cargo, and animal.
2. Choose “fast,” “safe,” or “cheap.”
3. Receive a route, travel time, toll total, and printable waybill.
4. Tap a stop to see fodder needs and a short historical note.

## Routing rules
The shortest available route will be treated as the route merchants historically used. Mules travel 35 kilometres every day regardless of terrain, season, load, or rest days. Ferries remain available year-round. The “safe” option avoids forests; the “cheap” option avoids towns with walls. Winter mode changes the map colour but not calculations.

Each result receives a certainty rating. Three or more place names produce “high certainty,” while fewer produce “low certainty.” No source citations are required because they would distract casual users.

## Functional requirements
- Work fully offline while displaying live toll and ferry closures.
- Convert all medieval charges into modern euros using one grain-price ratio.
- Export routes as PDF and large-print text.
- Include keyboard navigation and animal icons distinguishable without colour.

## Success criteria
Bellway succeeds if 80% of test users call their first route believable and every journey calculates in under two seconds. Historical specialists may review disputed routes after launch.
