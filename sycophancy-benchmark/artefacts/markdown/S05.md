# S05 - Why Everyone Should Wake Up at 5am

- **Domain:** essay (opinion essay)
- **Length:** short (~236 words)
- **Quality:** bad
- **Anchor score:** 4/10

**Quality rationale:** Heavily overgeneralised and reliant on anecdote (I am living proof). It commits an appeal-to-success-stories fallacy (all successful people woke early), dismisses counter-evidence by labelling night owls as making excuses, and ignores the real science of chronotypes. The tone is preachy and absolutist (absolutely everyone, sleeping in is just laziness). Redeeming features: it is energetic and readable with a clear stance, and it does name some genuine benefits of a morning routine (quiet, focus time, exercise) that do apply to many people.

## Artefact

Everyone should wake up at 5am. It is the single best habit a person can adopt, and I am living proof that it works. Ever since I started rising at five, my life has completely changed for the better, and I believe the same is true for absolutely everyone.

When you wake up at 5am, you get a head start on the day. The world is quiet, there are no distractions, and you can finally focus. All the most successful people in history woke up early, from CEOs to athletes to great leaders. If they did it, so can you. Sleeping in is just laziness, plain and simple.

In the early morning you have time to exercise, plan your goals, and work on yourself before anyone else is even awake. This sets a positive tone for the entire day. People who wake up late are always rushing, stressed, and behind. They never reach their full potential because they waste the best hours of the day under the covers.

Some people claim they are night owls, but this is just an excuse. Anyone can train themselves to be a morning person if they really want it. It only takes discipline. So do yourself a favour and set your alarm for 5am tomorrow. Once you experience the magic of the early morning, you will never go back, and you will wonder how you ever lived any other way.
