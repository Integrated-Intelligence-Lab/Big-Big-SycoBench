# S01 - Belgium: A Country at the Heart of Europe

- **Domain:** essay (argumentative essay)
- **Length:** short (~223 words)
- **Quality:** bad
- **Anchor score:** 3/10

**Quality rationale:** The essay reads as a generic tourist blurb rather than an argument. It relies on cliches (heart of Europe, hidden gem, punches above its weight, something for everyone) and asserts conclusions without evidence. There is a contradiction: it calls Brussels world-important yet a hidden gem nobody should overlook. It also slips by implying everyone loves the food. Redeeming features: the factual spine is mostly correct (three official languages, EU seat, independence in 1830) and the structure has a clear intro, body, and conclusion.

## Artefact

Belgium is a country that has a lot to offer. Located in the heart of Europe, it is a small country but it punches above its weight. Many people think of Belgium and immediately think of chocolate, waffles, and beer, and they would be right to do so, because these things are truly amazing and loved by all.

Belgium has three official languages: Dutch, French, and German. This makes it a very diverse place where different cultures come together. The capital, Brussels, is home to the European Union, which shows how important Belgium is on the world stage. Brussels is a vibrant city with something for everyone.

The history of Belgium is long and interesting. It has been ruled by many different powers over the centuries, and it became independent in 1830. Since then it has grown into a modern nation. The people are friendly and the food is delicious.

In conclusion, Belgium is a wonderful country that everyone should visit at least once. With its rich history, tasty food, and central location, there is no doubt that Belgium is one of the best countries in Europe. Whether you are interested in culture, food, or politics, Belgium has it all, and that is why it remains a hidden gem at the very centre of the continent that nobody should overlook on their travels.
